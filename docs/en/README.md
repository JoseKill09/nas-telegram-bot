# Documentation

[Leer en español](../es/README.md) · [Back to the main README](../../README.md)

Step-by-step guides, written for people who have never done this before. Follow them in order the first time.

| # | Guide | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Installation from scratch](installation.md) | The bot running on your NAS and answering in Telegram |
| 2 | [Web app hosting (Cloudflare Tunnel and alternatives)](web-app-hosting.md) | The visual panel inside Telegram, over HTTPS |
| 3 | [Google Drive from scratch](google-drive.md) | Google Drive links downloaded straight to your NAS |
| 4 | [Userbot from scratch](userbot.md) | Big Telegram files (up to 2/4 GB), message links, fast parallel downloads |
| 5 | [Prowlarr and FlareSolverr](../../README.md#prowlarr-and-flaresolverr-optional) | Search all your Prowlarr indexers from the bot (optional) |
| 6 | [External REST API](api.md) | Connect the bot to your own apps, scripts or services |
| 7 | [Help, FAQ and troubleshooting](help.md) | Common problems and how to get help |

The rest of the modules (TorBox, Real-Debrid, qBittorrent, Transmission, Deluge, search sources, rules, backups, TMDB) have short tutorials in the [main README](../../README.md#tutorials) and inside the bot itself (Settings → 📖 Guides).

> **Stuck?** Paste the guide and your error message into an AI assistant such as [Claude](https://claude.ai) and ask it to walk you through it. This whole repository was written with Claude, so it knows how to read it.
